# Comment, then create a plan

Hover the diff's gutter and press **+** to leave a comment for your coding
agent (drag over several lines first to cover them all). Tag it **Must fix**,
**Suggestion** or **Nit**, or press the mic in the comment box to say it.

**Create Plan** (the checklist on the Changes view) writes every comment to
`.review/review_<time>.md` and gives you the line to hand your coding agent.
